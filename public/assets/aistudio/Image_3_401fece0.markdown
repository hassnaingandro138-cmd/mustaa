## Brand & Style

This design system reimagines high-performance automotive luxury through an avant-garde lens: merging haute couture runway aesthetics with aerodynamic precision. Moving deliberately away from monotonous carbon fiber and heavy masculine clichés, the interface commands attention through crisp alabaster surfaces, luminous crystalline glass, and electric rose-quartz vibrancy.

- **Brand Personality**: Audacious, bespoke, aristocratic, magnetic. It presents hypercars and bespoke luxury grand tourers as sculptural fine art.
- **Target Audience**: Ultra-high-net-worth collectors, visionary executives, and tastemakers who demand uncompromising engineering paired with expressive, high-fashion styling.
- **Emotional Response**: Exhilaration, prestige, precision craftsmanship, and unapologetic glamor.
- **Design Movement**: Modern High-Fashion Minimalism fused with Crystalline Glassmorphism. The aesthetic utilizes generous optical white space, hairline borders, ultra-fine typography, and vibrant radiant-pink emissive highlights that mimic high-end bespoke vehicle paint finishes.

## Colors

The palette balances pristine, gallery-grade purity against calibrated shocks of chromatic pink and deep graphite precision.

- **Primary (`#db2777`) & Radiant Pink Accent (`#ec4899`, `#f43f5e`)**: Serves as the signature performance accent. Used for high-intent actions, telemetry markers, dynamic vehicle badges, active performance toggles, and radiant focus states.
- **Tertiary Surface Tint (`#fdf2f8`)**: A pale rose quartz veil used for ultra-subtle card backgrounds, elevated pill badges, and luminous structural separators that maintain warmth within a white-dominant space.
- **Neutral Dominance (`#0f172a` & `#1e293b`)**: Midnight graphite and deep slate provide authoritative, museum-level contrast for vehicle specifications, pricing figures, and display typography, preventing the composition from drifting into casual territory.
- **Base Canvas (`#ffffff`)**: Pure optical white canvas establishes pristine negative space reminiscent of an architectural glasshouse showroom.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes tension between haute couture geometry and high-tech instrumentation:

- **Headlines (Syne)**: Distinctive, structural, and sculptural. Conveys high fashion, architectural gravitas, and forward-looking extravagance in vehicle naming, series launches, and hero showroom titles.
- **Body (Hanken Grotesk)**: Ultra-clean, razor-sharp neo-grotesque delivering pristine readability across vehicle specs, client documentation, and bespoke bespoke configuration details.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Metrics (Space Grotesk)**: Monospaced sensibilities with wide tracking for horsepower, torque, 0-60 acceleration times, chassis numbers, and navigational wayfinding. Always presented in uppercase for technical prestige.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors an open architectural showroom floor: broad expanses, unhurried focal planes, and deliberate geometric alignment.

- **Grid Strategy**: 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`> 1024px`) with a maximum container limit of `1440px`. Transitions to an 8-column layout on tablets (`768px - 1023px`), and a 4-column stack on mobile (`< 767px`).
- **Showroom Cadence**: Wide outer canvas margins (`margin: 3rem`) frame hypercar imagery with the sanctity of fine art in a white-cube gallery.
- **Reflow Dynamics**: On handheld screens, complex horizontal vehicle specification matrices (e.g., V12, 780 BHP, 2.8s) convert into horizontally scrollable ticker carousels, while vehicle configurators collapse into sticky bottom glass panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through crystalline luminescence rather than traditional murky dropshadows:

- **Tonal Layers & Showroom Pedestals**: Surface levels transition from base `#ffffff` to elevated `#fdf2f8` (Rose Quartz tint), crowned by frosted glass panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) backed with a `24px` backdrop blur.
- **Radiant Ambient Glow**: Floating elements and focused hypercars cast faint, diffused chromatic pink ambient auras (`box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.16)`).
- **Hairline Luxury Borders**: Crisp, micro-engineered 1px boundaries (`rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.12)` or `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`) ground pristine cards, delivering jewelry-grade finish without heavy outlines.
- **Emissive State Elevation**: On hover, interactive vehicles and control pods project an energized rose neon reflection along bottom edges (`0 4px 20px rgba(244, 63, 94, 0.28)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces an architectural Soft (`roundedness: 1`) geometric silhouette. 

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`)**: Precision micro-components such as data tags, technical spec brackets, and telemetry chips retain sharp, calculated corners reflecting automotive engineering tolerances.
- **Intermediate (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem`)**: Applied to pristine showroom cards, luxury media viewports, and primary interactive buttons to soften interaction without diluting structural authority.
- **Contrasting Circular Elements (`rounded-full`)**: Exclusive to 360-degree rotation toggles, paint swatch samplers, and floating showroom audio controls to create focal dynamism against linear card frames.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary Radiant*: Gradient fill from `#db2777` to `#f43f5e`, sharp `0.5rem` radius, crisp white text in uppercase `label-lg`, and an ambient rose glow. On hover, the luminance flares with a `1.02` micro-scale.
  - *Secondary Showroom*: Pure `#ffffff` surface with a 1px rose-quartz border (`rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.3)`), dark slate text, and instant rose-tinted inner glow on hover.
  - *Tertiary Ghost*: Minimal slate typography with an animated rose underline indicator expanding from center.

- **Pristine White Cards & Showroom Pods**:
  - Built with `#ffffff` backgrounds, subtle hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.1)`), and soft `0.5rem` corners.
  - Feature generous inner padding (`space-xl` on desktop, `space-md` on mobile).
  - Include an upper metadata deck for model designation and production numbers in monospaced uppercase, followed by edge-to-edge cinematic vehicle viewports.

- **Chips & Specification Tags**:
  - Pill and soft-rectangle badges sporting `#fdf2f8` surface fills, micro 1px borders in `#f43f5e`, and dark slate/deep magenta micro-text (`label-sm`).
  - Key performance callouts (e.g., "0-60 MPH: 2.4s") highlight numerical values in vibrant `#db2777`.

- **Input Fields & Bespoke Inquiries**:
  - Pristine white backgrounds with inset graphite borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`).
  - Focus state transitions border to neon rose (`#ec4899`) backed by a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(236, 72, 153, 0.15)` focus ring.
  - Floating labels utilize `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking.

- **Checkboxes & Radios**:
  - Custom square-soft (`checkbox`) and concentric circular (`radio`) selectors.
  - Unchecked: 1.5px border in `#94a3b8`. Checked: solid vibrant rose (`#db2777`) fill with optical white interior glyph.

- **Lists & Spec Sheets**:
  - High-density comparison grids separated by hairline rose dividers (`rgba(219, 39, 119, 0.08)`).
  - Alternating telemetry labels (muted graphite) and performance metrics (bold slate with pink units).

- **Domain-Specific Components**:
  - *360° Interior/Exterior Visualizer Pod*: Transparent glass overlays displaying multi-axis camera coordinates, bespoke material swatches (leather, carbon weave, satin paint), and interactive hotspot beacons that pulse with radial rose rings.
  - *Engine Soundwave & Telemetry Strip*: Minimalist audio frequency visualizer rendered in thin rose-pink bars enabling prospective clients to audition exhaust profiles.
